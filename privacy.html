Gramwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

The short version

Gramwise has no account or login. Your food diary, profile, weight history and Health Connect imports stay on your phone, apart from copies you explicitly export and earlier Android system backups described below. Advanced AI sends only the photo, hint or description you choose for that action, not your stored diary or Health Connect records. The core tracker works without a network: the diary, the bundled food search, describing a meal in words, saved foods and manual entry. Gramwise runs one recognition service: an Advanced meal scan sends the whole meal photo and any optional hint you type or dictate, a label scan sends the label photo, and Advanced Describe sends the words you typed or dictated, never the audio. Our Worker stores none of those submissions and no diary; Google provider retention is described below; it keeps shared food-and-nutrition facts and pseudonymous operational records for quotas, subscription verification and replay protection. There is no on-device recognition, so a photo scan always sends the photo and cannot run without a connection. Advanced AI requires the paid Pro subscription; there is no free Advanced-AI allowance. Google Play Billing checks your subscription when the app starts, and for Free users only, Google's consent and AdMob SDKs may start after that check.

What stays on your device

Your profile (date of birth, height, sex, activity level, goals and targets), your food diary and meal notes, your weight and body-measurement history, your saved foods and templates, the meal photos you attach, and any data imported from Health Connect are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The one exception you control is the optional gallery album described under "Photos, meal scans and label scans". Food and nutrition values come from a read-only database bundled inside the app, so a food lookup needs no internet. A backup you create is written to a file and handed to your phone's share sheet, where you decide where it goes; it includes photos only if you turn on "Include food & meal photos". Gramwise never uploads an exported backup itself. This version excludes private app data from Android system backup and transfer; older system backups and gallery backups are described below. Local notifications and reminders are scheduled on your phone; any text shown on the lock screen is subject to your notification settings.

What leaves your device, and why

Some requests happen automatically. Network recipients necessarily receive your IP address and connection metadata. The recognition service uses your IP address through Cloudflare for short-window rate limiting and abuse prevention. When the app starts, and periodically when it returns to the foreground, it asks Google Play Billing whether the current Google account has an active Gramwise Pro subscription. For a Free user only, the Google consent and AdMob SDKs can then initialise, read the ads-consent state, show Google's consent form where required, and request the Today banner (see "Advertising and analytics"). A Pro subscriber's app may confirm the current Advanced-AI allowance with the recognition service using the Google Play purchase token. Other requests wait for you. "Search online" sends a search term or barcode number to Open Food Facts. An Advanced AI request sends the meal photo, label photo or meal description you chose to submit, together with your Google Play purchase token and the app's display language (a language tag such as `en` or `ru`, never a location or device identifier); if you added an optional meal hint on the approval screen, the app sends that hint with the photo. The service verifies the token with Google Play, stores a short-lived verdict and hashed quota counters keyed by a one-way hash, and returns a food name, brand where available, an estimated portion, calories and nutrients, all marked unverified for your review. When nutrition for a named branded or chain product is missing, the service may send only that food's name to Google Search for published facts; your image, hint, description and identifiers are not part of that search, and names produced by a scan with a hint are neither searched nor written to the cache. The service keeps a shared cache of food names and nutrition, never linked to a person. Advanced Describe sends the text you type or dictate: words, not a picture, and no audio. When Advanced is selected, finishing voice input automatically sends the complete text shown in the field after Advanced-AI consent; nothing is sent while recognition is active, and if your phone ends recognition without marking the last words final, the last text it displayed is included. Selecting Lite keeps the parse on your phone and sends no description; Lite parses entirely on the phone, reading the bundled database. Turning speech into text is done by your phone's own speech service, the same one your keyboard's voice typing uses; depending on the phone and its settings that service may process the audio on its provider's servers, and Gramwise never receives, stores, or logs your audio. To help it hear food words, Gramwise gives it a short fixed list of commonly misheard foods that is the same for everyone and contains nothing from your diary. Aside from an Advanced AI submission you choose and a backup you choose to share, Gramwise does not send your diary, profile, meal-photo library, weight or Health Connect data off the device.

Photos, meal scans and label scans

Photos you take or pick are saved in the app's private storage so they can appear next to your diary entries. Naming the food in a photo needs the recognition service: there is no on-device recognition, nothing is sent unless you start a scan, and with no connection a photo scan cannot run at all; nothing is queued or sent later. Advanced AI sends nothing until you accept the shared disclosure, shown from Settings → AI & recognition or your first Advanced action, and a scan also needs the Pro subscription. When you tap Scan, your whole meal photo is sent, uncropped, together with any optional meal hint you typed or dictated on the approval screen, so the AI can find the foods in it. It sees whatever you photographed, including the plate, the table and anything else in frame; point the camera at your meal, not at anything you would rather not send. You can dictate the hint with the mic beside it: your phone's own speech service turns your voice into text and Gramwise never receives, stores or logs the audio; only the words shown in the field are sent, and only when you tap Scan. The AI finds the foods in the whole frame on its own: there is no way to mark, crop or box a food as input to a scan. Post-scan box adjustments change local thumbnails only and are not submitted for recognition. The request also carries technical mode flags; connection metadata such as the IP address reaches the hosting provider. Portions and nutrition from a meal photo are AI estimates and can be wrong; the app asks you to check or correct them before logging. If a kitchen scale's display is in the picture, the AI reads the grams straight off it, but reading a display is still AI transcription and can misread the number or units; check it against the actual scale; there is nothing to switch on, a photo without a scale simply comes back without a reading, and if the food sits in a bowl on the scale the app says so because the reading may include the bowl's weight unless you tared it. A nutrition-facts label is a second photo mode: the label photo is sent to the same service to read the nutrition facts and transcribe the product name, serving size, calories and nutrients printed on it; transcription can be wrong, so you check it before logging. Both modes need a connection, count against the Pro allowance stated in the app, and there is no free Advanced-AI allowance. Each Advanced request carries your Google Play purchase token, which the service verifies with Google Play; the same token proves an active paid subscription. Gramwise does not send a Play Integrity token or hashed Android ID and keeps no device ledger. Our Worker retains no submitted image, meal hint, description or diary; Google provider retention is described under Retention; it retains only the shared food cache and the pseudonymous operational records described above. Photos leave your phone in one other way that you control: Settings → Data & backup → "Save meal photos to Gramwise" copies your meal photos into a Gramwise album in your phone's gallery. It is OFF unless you turn it on, Android asks for permission first, and nothing is sent off your phone by it; but a photo in your gallery is visible to other apps you have given photo access, and a photo-backup service such as Google Photos may upload it under your own account settings. Turning the switch off stops new copies; photos already in the gallery stay there for you to keep or delete.

Health Connect

If you choose Connect in Settings → Integrations and grant Android Health Connect permissions, Gramwise reads the weight, step and exercise-session data you permit. Sessions are reduced on your phone to a daily workout count and duration; no routes, heart rate, distance or workout calories are requested. Access is read-only: nothing is written back or sent to our recognition service, AI providers or advertisers. Imported weights support your weight history and local plan calculations; steps and workouts provide activity context and do not add calorie credit. Manual sync is available; Pro can sync automatically when the App returns to the foreground while connected. Disconnect stops future sync but does not delete imported records or revoke Android permissions. You can revoke each permission in Health Connect and use Remove imported data in Gramwise to delete imported records without deleting your manual entries; disconnect first to prevent reimport. Data held by other health apps or Health Connect is managed there separately. Health Connect data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models.

Advertising and analytics

Gramwise uses no analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK; local diagnostic counts stay on your phone and exclude images, diary entries and personal details. For Free users, Google AdMob can read the advertising ID, IP address and general device information to serve, cap and measure ads and, with your consent, personalise them. One banner can appear on Today and nowhere else. Where Google requires it, its consent form appears before personalised ads, and Gramwise Settings offers Google's privacy-options form when the SDK reports that it is required. Pro subscribers do not initialise the AdMob or consent SDK. Gramwise does not send your diary, profile, photos, weight or Health Connect data through the advertising SDK or to advertisers; Advanced AI submissions go to Google separately as disclosed above; Google's handling of AdMob and Play Billing data follows Google's privacy policy.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b)) for the core tracker and purchase verification; consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for optional Advanced AI submissions and Health Connect access; and explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)) for health information processed through those optional features. The AI disclosure explains the recipients and processing before you enable it; starting a scan or choosing Advanced Describe then selects the material to send. For Health Connect, choosing Connect and granting the specific Android permissions selects the health records to import and process locally. These choices are optional and separate from accepting the Terms. Turning Advanced AI off or disconnecting Health Connect withdraws permission for future processing through that feature; it does not recall a completed request, delete existing imports or cancel a subscription. You can still use the core tracker without these integrations. For advertising, consent (Article 6(1)(a)) covers personalisation and device storage or access where required; legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) cover basic ad delivery, security and fraud prevention only where applicable law and your consent or objection choices permit. Google's form identifies the applicable vendors, purposes and legal bases; limited or non-personalised ads do not mean no personal-data processing. Revisit privacy options to withdraw consent or object where the SDK offers them, and manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) are preventing abuse, enforcing allowances, replay protection, service security and responding to support requests. We minimise identifiers and retention for those purposes and you may object. We also process information needed to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)), such as handling statutory requests. Health information you keep only on your phone is used for the tracking and plan functions you choose; it is not made available to us for unrelated purposes.

Retention

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Our Worker does not store submitted images, hints, descriptions or diary content. Operational records are pseudonymous, not anonymous: they can distinguish requests from the same subscription without containing your name. A subscription verdict is fresh for 15 minutes, or 60 seconds for a recoverable denial; storage continues for a further 24 hours to handle upstream outages, giving at most 24 hours 15 minutes or 24 hours 1 minute after each write. A cached upstream failure lasts 45 seconds. Daily counters expire 48 hours after their last write; weekly counters and replay markers expire after 9 days. Shared food facts expire 90 days after their last write, not last read. IP-based rate limits use 60-second windows; these windows do not define Cloudflare's separate infrastructure/security retention. Google Vertex AI may cache inputs and outputs in memory for up to 24 hours and retain flagged prompts for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days, subject to its applicable settings and terms; we do not promise a project-level retention exemption. Google Search grounding retains its prompts, context and output for 30 days. Google states that Vertex AI customer data is not used to train its models without permission. See Google's retention documentation. Other providers retain their data under the linked policies. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve your request and meet legal obligations or establish or defend claims, then deleted. We do not have a remote copy of your diary to restore.

Security

The App uses Android's private storage and HTTPS for its own network requests. Android platform backup and device-transfer rules in this version exclude the App's private data; backups are created through the export and share controls you choose. Earlier versions enabled Android system backup, so a copy made under your device's backup settings may still exist in your Google account or on another device; an app update cannot erase those copies. Use your Android or Google backup controls to manage them. Private storage is protected by your device's access controls but is not a promise of separate app-level encryption or immunity to a compromised device. The Worker receives your raw purchase token to verify it with Google and stores a one-way hash as the key for operational records, not the raw token. The App keeps a local purchase credential for billing; do not include it in support requests. Exported backup files are not encrypted by Gramwise. Protect the file, destination and recipients; no storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We respond without undue delay and normally within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we explain it within that month. You may complain to UODO in Poland or your local supervisory authority without contacting us first. You can edit and delete local records and export the backup available in Settings → Data & backup without Pro. That backup includes diary, profile, weight history, saved foods and optional photos, but does not include imported daily step and workout summaries; manage or obtain the underlying activity records through Health Connect and the originating apps. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the App's local data, but not exported backups, gallery copies, earlier system backups, Health Connect's records or provider-held information. Manage those copies with their holders. Disconnect Health Connect and remove imported data to stop sync and remove its local copies. The service has no account directory, but its hashed records remain pseudonymous and are not automatically exempt from your rights. Contact us; we may need proportionate identifying information and will explain if we cannot locate a record. Do not send a raw purchase token or a copy of your health diary. Local plan calculations and automated entitlement decisions support the features described in the Terms; you can edit or disable the plan and ask us to review an access error. We do not use health data for advertising profiles.

Children

Gramwise is intended for adults: the Terms of Use require users to be 18 or older, and the app enforces this at the date-of-birth entry. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them; ads are requested as not child-directed with a maximum content rating of PG.
