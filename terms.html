Using the app

By using TallyBite you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. TallyBite is for adults making their own everyday food choices: you must be 18 or older to use it, and the app checks this when you enter your date of birth.

Not medical advice

TallyBite gives general nutrition information and estimates — it is not medical, dietetic, or other professional advice, and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating. If tracking food or calories harms your wellbeing, stop and seek support.

Estimates, not measurements

Food recognition, portion sizes, nutrition values, calorie targets, and projections are estimates. They can be wrong — that's why every entry is a proposal you confirm and can edit. Never rely on the app for allergy, ingredient-safety, or medication decisions; check the actual packaging or ask the kitchen.

Your data & backups

Your data lives only on this phone — there's no account and no server copy (see the Privacy Policy for the full picture). That also means backups are in your hands: uninstalling the app or losing the phone loses the data with it. Export a backup from Settings → Data & backup to keep your own copy.
